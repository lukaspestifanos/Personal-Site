Triptych Digital x Cantina

Forward Deployed Engineer · first engineer hired, sole technical hire on site

Cantina is a 440+ person AI character platform backed by a16z, Benchmark, Greycroft, and Founders Fund, founded by Sean Parker. I report to Triptych Management's acting CTO, sit in client communications and GTM planning, and turn an ambiguous distribution deal into shipped production systems.

  • PipelineCut production from six hours per video to thirty minutes, a 92% reduction, by replacing a manual sequential process with a parallel pipeline run from Slack, with quality scoring and selective retries so failed segments regenerate on their own.
  • EnginesBuild and operate the automated video production system: 60 engines that each generate a full episode end to end, up to 30 running concurrently.
  • ContinuityVideo models cap at 15 seconds and hold no state between calls. Built the layer that stitches eight or more generations into a coherent two-minute episode with consistent characters, sets, and story.
  • PlatformBuilt TripForce, the internal platform both the team and the client run on: 13 views over one backend, role-scoped so Cantina sees delivery reporting while Triptych sees production internals. Tracks 1,200+ episodes, 1,500+ graded clips, 123 accounts, and per-run cost against weekly contractual targets.
  • DataIndexed 5,900+ artifacts across Google Drive, Cloudflare R2, and the render service to reconcile what was produced against what shipped. Stood up a 750-entity self-wiring knowledge graph on GBrain (Postgres, pgvector) linking runs, engines, accounts, concepts, and failure signatures, kept current by recursive enrichment passes.
  • AgentsBuilt Slack-resident agents with persistent memory and shared context that became the primary way non-engineers run production. Rearchitected from process automation to agent-callable tooling to MCP servers, so Slack, SSH, direct API, and agent orchestration are interchangeable entry points to the same primitives.
